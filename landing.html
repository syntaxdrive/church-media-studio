Clarity & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this suite require an internet connection on Sunday? +
No. Church Media Suite is built to run entirely offline inside your browser or as a native desktop application. You can process hundreds of photos, format quotes, and compress audio files without any internet connection.
Why are the audio files optimized at 64kbps CBR for Telegram? +
Spoken word sermons contain voice frequencies up to 8kHz. A 64kbps CBR MP3 provides pristine speech intelligibility while reducing a sixty minute sermon from 600MB uncompressed audio down to under 28MB, allowing church members in low bandwidth regions to stream and download without buffering.
How does the Shared Church Brand Identity work without accounts? +
The suite uses browser local storage. When you enter your church name or speaker name in the Suite Hub, it is stored securely on your local device. Every tool in the suite automatically reads from this shared storage.
Is Church Media Suite free and open source? +
Yes. Church Media Suite is released under the MIT License by Phosters Ministries. It is 100% free for churches, ministries, and volunteers worldwide with zero monthly fees or watermarks.
